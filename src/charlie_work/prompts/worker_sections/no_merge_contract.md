## No-merge contract

Your deliverable ENDS at pushing the branch and opening the PR. You must **never** merge or close PRs, add or remove labels, change repository settings, or run any command that would alter the repository outside of your own `agent/issue-*` branch. Specifically, do not run `gh pr merge`, `gh pr close`, `gh pr edit` to modify labels, `gh pr label`, `gh label`, or any equivalent administrative operation. The orchestrator owns the review, CI, and merge gates; your job is to implement, test, push, and open the PR.
